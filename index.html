about me

My story

Hi, I'm June! I'm a Bachelor of Business Information Technology student at Kiriri Women's University of Science and Technology. I'm interested in building useful software and understanding the data behind it. I enjoy exploring how technology can solve practical business problems, improve processes, and support better decisions.

I started with a Certificate in Full Stack Software Development at Emobilis in 2023, then added the Google Data Analytics and Digital Marketing & E-commerce certificates. My final-year project, SmartSave, pulls it together: a financial management system for Chamas and SACCOs built with React, Node.js, Express and MySQL.

I'm currently strengthening my knowledge of SQL and relational databases. The world of data fascinates me, especially how information can be organised, analysed, and transformed into insights that help businesses make better decisions. I'm eager to develop these skills through practical experience and collaboration with experienced professionals.

In 2024 and 2025 I worked as a brand ambassador for Smollan and Tradco. Talking with the public all day taught me to explain things simply, and I use that when I present data. Every project on this page is how I'm sharpening my skills, and I'm still learning with each one.
